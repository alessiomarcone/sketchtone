Knobs shape

    Draw a line to make a sound

    Left to right is time. Higher up is a higher note. Thicker lines play louder. Try the shapes and the spray too.

    Etch mode

    Left to right is time, top to bottom is pitch from high to low. With the keyboard: arrow keys move the pen, hold Shift to move faster, Enter starts and finishes a line, Escape cancels it. Space always plays or pauses.

        Drums

        Draw the red energy line: high = busy drums, low = sparse, a gap = silence. Or tap a style below.

        Left to right is time, as on the canvas. The red line is the drum energy: higher means busier drums. Parts with no line are silent. The dots are the hits the drummer generates. With the keyboard: Left and Right arrows move one step, Up and Down raise or lower the energy there, Delete makes a gap, Enter puts the line back.

        Project

        Your sketch saves itself in this browser while you work. Keep versions to go back in time, or a file to back up and share.

        Versions

          Project file

          A .sketchtone file holds the whole sketch: drawing, sounds, effects, drums and settings.

          Export

          Render the loop to a WAV file as long as you want, with the project as it is now: BPM, layers, mutes, effects, voice and drums. It renders faster than real time, no recording needed.

          Length

          Record, in the transport, still captures what you play live, knob moves included.

          Privacy

          • Your work stays on your device. Drawings, versions and settings are kept in this browser's local storage. No account, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking.
          • Sound files are made in your browser. Exports and recordings are created on your computer and saved straight to it. Nothing is uploaded.
          • MIDI only when you ask. The browser asks for MIDI access when you press Connect MIDI. Sketchtone only reads your controller.
          • No microphone, no camera.
          • No third parties. Everything, fonts included, comes from this site only, and the browser is told to refuse anything else.
          • Hosting. The site is served by Netlify, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested).
          • Delete anytime. Settings → Delete all saved data removes everything Sketchtone stored in this browser.

            Pads
